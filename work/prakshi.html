02 Collection copy & page design

Three sentences and one photograph.

The Evermore page opens with a shoulder crop, the collection name set in wide capitals, and a single paragraph that says what the locket does. No feature list, no scroll-triggered claims.

Copy sits on skin, not on gold
Text is placed in the soft right third of the frame so the locket and chain stay completely unobstructed.
One paragraph, centred
Diamond-studded 18k gold lockets designed to hold photographs, zodiacs, initials and charms. That is the whole pitch.
Serif for the name only
EVERMORE is the only wide-tracked element on the page, which is what makes it read as a collection rather than a product.
Twelve zodiacs, one frame
Each locket is shot on the same grey card at the same scale, so the row reads as a set and the birthstone is the only variable.
Name, then price
Two centred lines under each piece. No badges, no hover overlays, no discount language on a fine-jewellery page.
Charms sold as companions
The photo locket, the zodiac plate and the enamel charms are merchandised together, because that is how they were shot.
The hero object
A photograph you can wear.

The pavé-set photo plate is the reason the collection exists, so it gets the only close-crop still-life in the shoot — held in an open palm, at true scale, with a real family picture inside it rather than a placeholder.

03 The launch shoot

Studio for the product, daylight for the mood.

Two setups in one day. A dark studio for necklines and metal detail, then an outdoor set for the campaign frames — same model, same jewellery, two completely different uses.

Neckline crops for the store
Chin to collarbone, black background, one light. These frames carry every product page and every zodiac variant.
Hands for the stack
Rings, bangles and bracelets are shot as one arrangement, so the site can sell four pieces from a single photograph.
Real skin, real light
Minimal retouching on the hands. At this price point the buyer is checking how the piece sits, not how flawless the frame is.
04 Video & campaign set

Fine jewellery, worn on an ordinary afternoon.

The film for the launch is built from this set: a coconut, an ice cream, a green corrugated wall. Nothing staged as luxury, everything shot in hard afternoon sun — so a 200,000-rupee locket reads as something you actually put on.

05 Our Tribe

Customers, in their own photographs.

A rotating cover flow
Real customer pictures, angled away at the edges so the centre frame is always the one you read.
Unretouched on purpose
Weddings, parties, phone snaps. It sits directly under the studio campaign and earns the collection its credibility.
