02 The library — shopping by description

Describe the room. The library answers.

Filters assume you already know what you want. Most rug buyers know the evening they are planning, not the knot count. So the search bar takes a sentence — the mood, the light, the budget — and returns a selection from today’s racks with the reason attached.

01
A sentence, not a filter
“A warm wool ground for a countryside dinner” is a real query, and the seeded prompts teach people they can talk like that.
02
A thread you can pick up
Past searches stay on the page as a journey. No account, no login — it lives in the browser.
03
Camera in the same bar
Photograph the floor instead of describing it. One input handles both, so nobody has to find a second feature.
04
It follows you down
The bar docks to the bottom of every page, including the product page, so the conversation never restarts.
