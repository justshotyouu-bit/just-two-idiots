02 The app — AI at the centre

The room decides which rug you see.

One photo of your floor, and the app reads the room’s colours, scores the whole inventory against it, and tells you why each rug fits. The analysis runs on the phone — the photo never leaves the device.

01
Snap your room
One photo and the app reads its colours to find rugs that belong. Analysed on your phone — the photo never leaves your device.
02
Design Studio
Six questions about the space — walls, light, mood, size — and every rug comes back with a fit score and the reason why.
03
See in your room
Any rug in the collection, at true size, on your real floor through the camera. One tap, powered by AR Quick Look.
