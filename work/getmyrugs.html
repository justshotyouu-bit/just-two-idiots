GetMyRugs

A heritage rug retailer's storefront, rebuilt around fifty years of family weaving, plain-language AI search, and a loyalty program worth sticking around for.

Services

Scope
Storefront + search
Category
Handmade Rugs — eCommerce
Overview

GetMyRugs sells wool, silk, and jute rugs woven by the same families for nearly fifty years — a story with real weight that wasn't showing up on the site. The rebuild leads with it: a "Woven by families, not factories" hero, verified reviews front and center, and a search bar that lets shoppers describe what they want in plain words instead of digging through filters.

A rug is a decades-long purchase. The site needed to earn that kind of trust in one scroll.

What we focused on
01
Heritage-led homepage
Story and reviews placed above the fold, not buried in an About page.
02
Plain-language search
An AI-assisted search bar for shoppers who don't think in filters.
03
Reserve membership
A loyalty tier for repeat collectors, with order tracking built in.
