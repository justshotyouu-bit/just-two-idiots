Overview

justshotyou is Krishan's personal photography and filmmaking portfolio. Instead of a conventional grid, photos sit scattered and layered like a physical mood board, with a simple Photos, Films, About structure underneath. Built on Next.js and kept intentionally minimal, so the work stays the focus instead of the site around it.

A photography site shouldn't out-design the photos. This one mostly gets out of the way.
