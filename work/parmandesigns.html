Hand made terrazzo — Delhi

02 The shoot

Two founders, dressed in black, standing in their own material.

Param and Manuj wear black in every frame, so the colour in the picture is always terrazzo. We shoot the same studio monthly — sample wall, slab shelf, the tiled floor — and rotate which surface leads.

One wardrobe rule
Black on black, every shoot. Frames from different months cut together as one campaign.
Studio as a set
No location hire. The sample wall, the block shelf and the tiled floor are three backdrops already on site.
Disc wall — every finish in the catalogue, hung as a colour chart. Shot as the closing frame of the set.
03 The cover

Design Digest Delhi, shot in-house.

The cover frame came out of a regular monthly shoot, not a separate commission. Same studio, same black wardrobe, one composition held back for it.

Space left for the masthead
Founders low in the frame, white wall above. The cover logo and the Parman mark both land in clean space.
A quote, not a caption
One paragraph in the founders’ own words carries the story, set ragged-right into the bottom corner.
Props from the catalogue
Blue planter, speckled stools, rough-cast pedestals — every object on the cover is a product you can order.
“We see concrete not as cold, but as a story — one we shape with hands and intention.”
Param Deswal & Manuj Shukla
04 Product photography

Two angles, grey sweep, no styling.

Catalogue standard
The inlay is the product.

Brass grid, black chips and hand-cut symbols only read from directly above, so every piece is shot twice — one three-quarter view for the shelf presence, one plan view for the pattern. Same seamless grey, same light, so the whole catalogue sits together on a page.
