04 How we market it

Borrowed taste, then proof.

A founder quote opens the section, a wall of verified customer reviews closes it. The lamp is shown twice — once styled, once in somebody’s actual bedroom.

01
Creator endorsement
One portrait, one sentence, one product shot. Cut once, reused across the homepage, the PDP and paid social.
02
Real rooms, unretouched
Customer photos run beside studio frames. Warm, grainy, badly lit — and far more persuasive than the studio set.
03
Editorial numbering
01–04 over a colour-blocked grid turns a bestseller list into a spread. Same layout, new products each month.
04
Gifting season
Festive push built from the same three colours — boxes, store window and an app takeover shipped as one kit.
