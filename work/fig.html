03 The creative library

04 How we run it

Borrowed taste, then proof.

A creator opens the funnel, a wall of verified buyers closes it. The lamp is shown twice — once styled, once in somebody’s actual bedroom — and the pair is tested against each other rather than argued about.

Paid partnership
“So pretty, want one in every size.”
One portrait, one sentence, one product shot. Whitelisted through the creator’s own handle so it runs as a paid partnership rather than a repost, then cut down into statics for retargeting.
