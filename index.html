Who we are

The people behind the work

Just Two Idiots didn't start in a boardroom — it started in a classroom, where Krishan and Manish became friends in class one and have stayed that close for 22 years since. Krishan is a creative director and professional photographer who runs Just Shot You across Delhi and Gurgaon; Manish is an IIIT-trained engineer who'd rather ship something real than talk about it. We handle every project end to end, together, so brands move fast without cutting corners and meet this AI moment with real taste — growing alongside every client, always showing up with a faster, sharper version of the work.
